Music

The music I listen to is pretty varied and mostly changes based on what I'm doing on my PC. Most of the time, I have rap, hip-hop, and R&B playing in my headphones. I listen to a lot of artists from this scene, like Future, Don Toliver, Lil Baby, and Lil Durk, but also older stuff like 2Pac. Still, I'm not stuck on just one genre. Some days I completely switch it up and put on Sade for a calmer vibe, or I go for electronic music, listening to tekk and popcorn music, especially tracks by David Deejay. But when I need to focus on a university assignment or spend hours in the terminal and lyrics become distracting, I switch straight to instrumentals. I prefer something very chill, like "snowing like it's the end of the world," that just plays in the background without breaking my focus.
